# Curriculum Acquisition and Rights

Maintain continuous acquisition functions: University Scout, Practice-Set Scout, Dataset Scout, Public Records Scout, International Scout, Synthetic Factory, Adversarial Factory, Rights/Provenance Gate. Reuse existing agents where possible.

Prefer open educational resources, lawful public records, appropriately licensed datasets/practice material and fully synthetic data. Do not indiscriminately scrape private/copyrighted accounting records.

Each retained source records origin/reference, use/license basis, transformation/retention rights, source date, checksum and curriculum classification.

Synthetic Factory starts from known accounting truth and generates realistic source documents. Adversarial Factory creates controlled variants: blur, rotation, compression, photos, faded receipts, missing pages, duplicates, conflicts, multilingual/localized formats, date/currency variation, table fragmentation and handwriting where appropriate.

Acquisition should keep Hermes supplied with new work while preserving sealed-truth separation.
