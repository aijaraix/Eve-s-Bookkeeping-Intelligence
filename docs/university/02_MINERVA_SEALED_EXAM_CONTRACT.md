# Minerva Sealed Examination Contract

Minerva/Red Team establishes deterministic ground truth before Blue Team execution whenever truth is knowable. Sealed answers are inaccessible to solver contexts: no shared answer files, prompt leakage, database shortcuts or expected-result APIs.

Blue Team receives only material a simulated customer could legitimately provide.

After completion, Minerva grades independently across:
source completeness; extraction; evidence; canonicalization; accounting; reconciliation; clarification behavior; books; rendered UI; deliverables; reverse lineage; tenant/security behavior.

Correct abstention is a pass when evidence is genuinely insufficient. Fabrication and silent information loss fail.

Unknown/public cases without complete truth must be independently verified, augmented with synthetic truth, or explicitly marked non-deterministic; Minerva may not pretend an answer is known.

Maintain hidden holdouts to prevent overfitting. Solver, examiner, proposer and promotion authority remain separated.
