# Eve University — Raw-Input Activation

Status: CONTROLLING DIRECTIVE
Baseline: 30cf8c72b6fb7a6bd270eec55e61a5196851e67c

## Mission
Prove that Eve can accept the raw, messy information a bookkeeping/accounting team receives and transform it into defensible books, workpapers and professional outputs with complete evidence lineage.

The target is **100% accountable disposition**, not fabricated 100% extraction. Every relevant item must be correctly understood/reconciled, correctly classified irrelevant, or explicitly escalated for missing evidence. Silent loss and unsupported invention fail.

## Product-path rule
University is not a parallel demo. Academy tenants use the same authenticated customer product: workspace, Documents/upload, intake, queue, extraction, evidence, canonical facts, accounting, reconciliation, Exceptions/PBC, Reports/Report Wizard, downloads, support, and owner views. Direct DB/backend shortcuts do not certify customer behavior.

## Physical audit first, execution immediately
Inventory existing Hermes, Academy, Minerva, Darwin, Learning Dean, promotion authority, workforce registry, browser/customer simulator, Document IR, AnyDocParser, HybridExtraction, OCR, spreadsheet/table extraction, evidence, canonicalization, accounting, reconciliation, PBC, reporting, deliverables, lineage, internal audit, multilingual/model routing, capacity recovery and regression.

For every worker/component classify: OPERATIONAL, PARTIALLY_OPERATIONAL, DECLARED_ONLY, MISSING, BLOCKED. A registry entry or dashboard card is not physical execution evidence.

Do not wait for a giant audit to finish. Once sealed Red/Blue separation and safe Academy tenancy are verified, launch the first cohort.
