# 34 — Gemini Supervised First-Company Fix/Restart & Release-Nine Directive

## Owner objective

Once one clean company completes with no midstream intervention, release nine additional real companies to Eve's own autonomous scheduler and exit active supervision.

## Mandatory preflight correction before any cohort starts

Current canonical source must be inspected for and corrected if physically present. The following implementation patterns are release blockers:

1. **Production orchestration may not replace Document Intelligence with local regex/counting logic.** A production orchestrator must call the canonical Document Intelligence/Universal IR path and consume its persisted output. It may not simply read HTML itself, count tags, or derive headline values independently.
2. **No fixed financial fallback values.** Assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, income, cash flow or other material values may not be seeded from known historical companies or constants when extraction is missing.
3. **No denominator inflation.** Patterns such as `Math.max(actualCount, expectedMinimum)` may not manufacture source-inventory/extraction completeness.
4. **No hard-coded Minerva score or audit pass.** Evaluation results must come from actual independent evaluation.
5. **No pseudo-swarm.** An orchestrator callback that synchronously returns hard-coded output for HERMES/LEDGER/EUCLID/VERITAS/ATHENA/CLARA/QUINN/SENTINEL/LEXICON is not nine agent executions.
6. **No fabricated PBC/customer responses.** A public-company test may not claim that bank confirmations, trial balances or other PBC responses were received unless actual evidence exists.
7. **Browser actions must physically occur.** Launching Chrome and then recording strings describing file selection/upload is not a customer journey. The browser must manipulate the real DOM/file input, submit the product request, and observe the real network/server response.
8. **Navigation failure is failure.** Do not catch `page.goto` or selector failures and record a synthetic success such as `Loaded locally (Direct DOM runtime)`.
9. **Hash continuity must be measured at physical boundaries.** Do not assign the staging hash to browser/intake/IR hash fields without independently measuring the received/persisted bytes.
10. **Blind selection cannot be a preconfigured ten-company array.** If the trial is called blind, select eligible registrants at runtime. A fixed list is allowed only when explicitly labeled owner-defined cohort.
11. **Proof levels start unverified.** Do not initialize cohort state with `BROWSER_VERIFIED`/`PRODUCT_VERIFIED`/`AUTHORITATIVE_SOURCE_VERIFIED` before evidence exists.
12. **Production entrypoint reachability must be real.** Newly created engines must be reachable from the deployed production UI/server/worker/scheduler path. Detached modules and test-only imports do not satisfy implementation.

Do not start Company 1 until the release blockers above are absent from the authoritative production path and the corrected commit has been pushed and physically deployed.
